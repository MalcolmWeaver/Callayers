CALLAYERS

Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026

Callayers is a Windows app for creating cards and annotations to show alongside your webcam in video calls. This policy covers the Callayers website and the current tester release. For privacy or sign-in questions, contact malcolm@callayers.com.

Google sign-in and your account

Callayers uses Google sign-in and Firebase Authentication. Sign-in requests your Google account identity, email address and basic profile information, which can include your name and profile photo. Google and Firebase process this information to sign you in. Callayers uses your account identifier and verified email address to recognize your account, display your signed-in email and check access to the app. It does not request access to Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar or your contacts.

Your sign-in credentials are saved on your computer, protected by Windows, so you can stay signed in. Our account service stores your account identifier, access status, account creation date and daily AI usage count. Your account identifier also connects the product analytics described below to your account.

Your saved work and webcam

Cards, imported images, annotations and workspace settings are saved on your computer. Signing out keeps that work. People using the same Windows profile can access it after signing in, including with a different Google account.

Callayers combines your webcam, cards and annotations on your computer. When you select the Callayers camera in another app, that app receives the video output and handles it under its own privacy policy. Callayers does not send webcam footage to its AI or product analytics services.

Creating and revising cards with AI

When you ask AI to create or revise a card, Callayers sends your request and, for revisions, the selected card's content to our service and OpenAI to generate the result. Any personal information you include in that text is included in the request. Imported image files stay on your computer and are not uploaded for AI revisions.

Our account service records the request's account identifier, status and timing to manage usage and prevent duplicate requests. It does not save your prompts or generated card content in its account records. OpenAI processes the submitted content under its own data handling policies.

Product analytics

Callayers sends product usage events to PostHog to help us understand how the app is used. These include app session starts, card and annotation creation, showing and hiding cards, and camera preview and output changes. Events include timestamps, app version, session and card identifiers, an account-based identifier and, when detected, the name of the app using the Callayers camera, such as Zoom or a browser.

These events do not include prompts, card content or names, imported image files, annotation text, email addresses, device identifiers, webcam footage or the websites you visit. There is no analytics-off setting in this tester release.

Services that handle information

Google and Firebase provide sign-in and account services; Google Cloud hosts our account and AI request service; OpenAI processes AI requests; PostHog receives product analytics; and Cloudflare hosts the website and downloads. Requests to these services can expose ordinary connection information, such as an IP address. The app also contacts our services to check for updates.

Retention and your choices

Saved work stays on your computer until you remove it. You can delete cards and clear annotations in the app. Signing out removes the app's saved sign-in credentials, but does not delete your account records, analytics or saved work.

AI request status records are scheduled for deletion after 24 hours; cleanup may take longer. Account records and product analytics have no automatic deletion deadline set by the app. Service providers may retain information under their own policies.

You can review or remove Callayers access in your Google Account connections. Removing Google access does not delete data already saved by Callayers. To ask about your information or request deletion of account or analytics data, email malcolm@callayers.com. There is no in-app account deletion control in this tester release.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page when these practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.

Privacy Policy

Updated October 14, 2026

This policy explains how Callayers handles information when you use our website and Windows tester release. For questions or privacy requests, email malcolm@callayers.com.

Your account

When you sign in, your sign-in provider processes your account identity, email address and basic profile information, which can include your name and profile photo. Callayers uses your account identity and verified email address to sign you in, show which account you are using and check access to the app. We do not request access to your email messages, contacts, calendar or files in your online account.

We keep account information and AI usage counts to manage access and daily limits. Your computer remembers your sign-in until you sign out.

Your work and camera

Your cards, imported images, annotations and workspace settings are saved on your computer. Signing out does not delete them. Anyone using the same Windows profile can access that saved work after signing in.

Your camera view is combined with your cards and annotations on your computer. When you choose the Callayers camera in a calling app, that app receives the video and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not send your webcam footage for AI processing or usage analytics.

AI requests

When you create or revise a card with AI, your request and any selected card content are sent to an AI service to produce the result. This includes any personal information you put in that text. Imported image files are not uploaded for AI revisions.

We keep a short-lived record of each request's status to manage usage, but do not save your prompts or generated content in your account records. The AI service may retain submitted information under its own policies.

Usage information

We collect information about how Callayers is used to help improve it. This includes when you start the app, create content, show or hide cards, and use camera preview or output. These records include the app version, activity times, identifiers that connect activity to your account and cards, and, when detected, which app uses the Callayers camera.

Usage records do not include your prompts, card content or names, image files, annotation text, email address, webcam footage or websites you visit. This tester release does not have a setting to turn off usage analytics.

How information is shared

We use service providers to handle sign-in, host the website and downloads, process AI requests and analyze usage. They receive the information needed for those services. Visiting the website, downloading the app and using online features can also share connection information, such as your IP address, with the services handling those requests.

Keeping and removing information

Saved work remains on your computer until you remove it. You can delete cards and clear annotations in the app. Signing out removes your saved sign-in, but does not delete your saved work, account information or usage records.

AI request status records are scheduled for deletion after one day, though cleanup may take longer. Account information and usage records are not automatically deleted after a fixed period. Service providers may retain information under their own policies.

You can remove Callayers access through your sign-in account's connected apps settings. This does not delete information already held by Callayers. To request deletion of account information or usage records, email malcolm@callayers.com. Account deletion is not available within this tester release.

Policy updates

We will update this page when our privacy practices change. The date above shows when it was last revised.

TESTER RELEASE

Privacy

Callayers saves cards, images, annotations and workspace state on your computer. Signing out keeps that local work. Accounts using the same Windows profile can access those saved cards after signing in.

Sign-in and AI

Google and Firebase provide sign-in and account checks. When you create or revise a card with AI, the prompt and relevant card content are sent to the backend and its AI provider. Imported image bytes remain on your computer; revision uses local image references. Callayers does not send webcam footage to the AI service.

Basic product analytics

Callayers sends basic authenticated session, creation, card application/removal and camera transition events to PostHog. Events use an account-based identifier and a best-effort calling-app name, such as a browser name. Analytics do not include prompts, card content or names, image files, annotation text, email addresses, device identifiers, webcam footage or browsing sites. There is no telemetry-off setting in this tester release.

Downloads and updates

Cloudflare hosts the website and downloads. The installed app checks for authenticated updates and installs them when safe. Those requests expose ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, to the hosting service.

Installation & updates

Open the ZIP, move Callayers into Applications, and open it there. Sign in, allow webcam access, and enable Callayers Camera when macOS asks. Choose your webcam in Callayers, then choose Callayers as the camera in your calling app. Release builds check for updates automatically.
